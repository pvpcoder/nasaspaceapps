How is this calculated?

This is a simple, transparent score for the demo, not a validated fire model.

The sensor box measures temperature, crew presence (motion sensor) and whether the hatch is closed (ultrasonic). Oxygen is set with a dial to simulate the habitat’s air. Its switch triggers suppression, and a servo closes the vent on a red alarm.

    Where NASA has tested, and where it hasn’t

    Every dot is one test run in orbit. The ring is your habitat; the shaded band is the oxygen range planned for Moon bases.

    NASA’s Saffire-VI burned fabric and acrylic at 29–31% oxygen and about 7.9 psi in its final 2023–24 flight. Those results aren’t in the PSI data yet. NASA report

    No lunar-gravity data in PSI yet. NASA’s LUCI burned fabric and acrylic in simulated lunar gravity (spinning New Shepard rocket, normal air) in 2025; FM2 on the Moon is planned for late 2026.

    On Earth

    The same physics applies wherever air is oxygen-enriched on Earth, such as hospital oxygen therapy and hyperbaric chambers. NASA fire research has also fed into everyday safety equipment, including smoke detectors, firefighter gear and water-mist extinguishers.
