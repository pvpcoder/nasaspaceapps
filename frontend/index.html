About the sensor box

The sensor box measures temperature, crew presence (motion sensor) and whether the hatch is closed (ultrasonic). Oxygen and smoke are set with dials to simulate the habitat’s air and a smoke detector. A buzzer sounds at Warning level, and the switch silences it. Its switch triggers suppression, and a servo closes the vent when the temperature rises above the room’s normal (more sensitive at higher oxygen). The heat threshold is scaled down so a warm hand can trigger it in the demo.

    Outside PSI: what NASA has learned about lunar gravity

    None of this is in the PSI database yet. In lunar gravity, flames spread more slowly but can survive at lower oxygen, so Earth’s standard flammability test may not be conservative for a Moon base.

    The same physics on Earth

    The same physics applies wherever air is oxygen-enriched on Earth, such as hospital oxygen therapy and hyperbaric chambers. NASA fire research has also fed into everyday safety equipment, including smoke detectors, firefighter gear and water-mist extinguishers.
